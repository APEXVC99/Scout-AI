# Launching Scout AI

The best venture capital funds have one thing in common: they see more deals than anyone else. Not because they're smarter — because they have the headcount. An army of analysts, associates, and sourcing partners combing through databases, tracking founder movements, and sending cold emails. Every. Single. Day.

But here's the thing: the solo GP running a $5M fund? The angel syndicate lead who's also a full-time operator? They don't have that army. They have themselves, a laptop, and whatever time they can carve out between board meetings.

**That's why we built Scout AI.**

## What it does

Scout AI deploys autonomous AI agents that do the work of a junior analyst — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Here's the full loop:

- **Discovery** — Agents scrape startup databases, RSS feeds (TechCrunch, VentureBeat), and track founder movements. Every morning at 6am UTC, a fresh batch of companies lands in your pipeline.

- **Analysis** — GPT-4o builds investment theses and scores each company against your criteria. Not just keyword matching — actual semantic analysis using embeddings.

- **Deal Memos** — Auto-generated one-pagers with SWOT analysis, team background, market assessment, and a clear investment recommendation. The kind of memo you'd expect from a well-trained associate.

- **Outreach** — Personalized founder emails, drafted and ready to review. Edit, approve, send — all from your dashboard.

Scout AI bridges that gap.
